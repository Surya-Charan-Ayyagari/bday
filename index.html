Before any of this

Two kids who kept crossing paths.

Somewhere in this school, we sat in the same rooms, on the same days — with no idea what either of us would become to the other.

2017 · first message

A classmate. Then a notification.

We weren't close back then — barely knew each other. Then Facebook put your name in front of me again, and for some reason, I said hi.

2018 · still talking

Somehow, it never really stopped.

Conversations that used to end quickly started running long. I didn't have a word for it yet.

2019 · closer

Daily, not occasional.

By now I looked forward to your name on my screen more than I probably let on.

2020 · the ask

I said it. You said no.

This was the year I told you how I felt. You turned me down — and neither of us knew yet that it wasn't the end of the story.

sent · seen · no reply
2021 · just friends

Patience, disguised as friendship.

We went back to talking like nothing had happened. Except it had — I just kept it to myself a little longer.

December 2022 · first time, in person

At your sister's engagement, you stopped being a name on a screen.

Years of typing, and then you were just — there. Even better in person than every version I'd imagined.

April 2023 · us, officially

The month "maybe" finally became "yes."

This was the first birthday I got to celebrate as yours, properly. Finally, ours.

2024 · one year in

Proof that waiting was worth it.

A full year into us, and every birthday since feels like more evidence of the same thing.

2025 · not our best day

This one didn't go the way I planned.

I wanted this birthday to be something special, and it just didn't come together the way I'd hoped. I'm sorry for that. You deserved better — and you'll get it, starting now.

Still to come

To every birthday we haven't gotten to yet.
